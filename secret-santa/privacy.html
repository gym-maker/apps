Privacy Policy

Secret Santa Draw: Office Gift Exchange (Android app com.sendthisinstead.secretsanta)
Effective date: November 22, 2026

Summary

This app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

The names you add, any exclusion pairs, the draw results, the budget, the exchange date and the optional group name are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone so the app can remember them between sessions. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. The saved data is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfer.

No internet access, accounts, ads or analytics

The app does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data over the network. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Sharing and links

If you tap Share plan, the app hands a short text (group name, budget, date and participant names, but never the matches) to the Android share sheet. You choose which app receives it, and that app's own privacy policy applies. If you tap Get the printable organizer kit, the app opens a web page in your browser; the app itself sends nothing.

Deleting your data

Use Clear draw and remove names inside the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it from Android Settings. This permanently deletes everything the app saved.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
